rock climbing and the slow business of building confidence

Rock Climbing · Adventure

I got into climbing because I was interning in Atlanta, didn't know many people, and needed something to do. That's it.

Every route is a problem. The holds are there, the sequence isn't, and you either figure it out or you don't. You can't fake your way through it.

What surprised me was how fear shifts over time. Not that it disappears — but at some point you've fallen enough times that falling starts to feel like information rather than failure. You build up a record of figuring it out, and eventually you just kind of trust it.

The people I've met through climbing are some of the best I've come across. They'll cheer you through a move you've been stuck on for weeks without making it a whole thing.
