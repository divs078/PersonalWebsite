Jan 2023 – Jan 2024

700 campus orgs, one scheduling nightmare, and a team that actually shipped

Student Government Association · Georgia Tech

I'll be honest — when I first took on the IT Team Lead role at the Student Government Association, I didn't fully know what I was getting into. I knew it was a technical role. I knew I'd be managing a team. But this was one of my first leadership positions in college and I didn't know what to expect. I didn't anticipate how much I'd learn about building software that real people actually depend on.

Our project: an interview scheduling platform for Georgia Tech's ~700 campus organizations. If you've ever tried to coordinate interview slots across dozens of applicants, you know the chaos. Spreadsheets. Missed emails. Scheduling conflicts. We set out to fix that.

I led a team of 10+ students through an agile workflow. We held sprints, ran standups, and iterated constantly based on feedback from the org leaders who were our actual users.

What I'm most proud of isn't any specific feature. It's that we shipped something functional and usable. In a student org context, that's not a given. It takes real coordination, a lot of patience, and the willingness to cut scope when you need to.

That year taught me how to lead without authority — to motivate volunteers, manage competing priorities, and keep momentum going even when people are juggling midterms. I came out of it a better product thinker and a much better collaborator.
