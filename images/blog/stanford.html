a room full of women who chose to lead

Stanford GSB · Women's Leadership Conference

What you can't get from a recap is the energy in the room. The Stanford GSB Women's Leadership Conference brings together people at every stage — students, founders, executives, researchers — and what you feel sitting in that space is something like possibility made concrete. These are not hypothetical career paths. These are people who built them.

The conversations that mattered most happened in the in-between moments. A woman I met at lunch who'd pivoted out of consulting to start a climate company. A founder who talked honestly about the years it took before anything worked. A panel discussion that turned into a real argument about what "having it all" even means, and whether that's a question worth asking.

I left with a longer reading list, a few new contacts, and a clearer sense of what I actually want. Not a specific title or trajectory — more of a feeling. A direction. That's harder to articulate but easier to act on.
