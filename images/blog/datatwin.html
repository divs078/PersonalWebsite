you are your data: building a mirror that shows you what algorithms see

Data Twin · ML Project

Every search, every video, every scroll at 2am is a data point. Together they're a pretty accurate model of you — what you care about, what you'll do next. You never get to see that model. Companies do.

Data Twin lets you run the same analysis yourself. Upload your Google Takeout or TikTok export and it classifies your activity, computes behavioral metrics, and gives you a profilability score — how predictable your behavior is based on your history. Nothing is stored. Everything runs in memory and gets discarded after the response.

How it works

React frontend, FastAPI backend, Python ML pipeline. Each item in your export gets classified into one of eight topics — tech, fashion, politics, finance, self-improvement, entertainment, health, travel — using a fine-tuned DistilBERT model, with a zero-shot BART fallback when needed.

The pipeline then computes metrics on your sequence: how diverse your topics are, how stable your attention is, how often you switch, how deep you go. A first-order Markov chain trained on 80% of your history predicts the remaining 20% — that accuracy is your profilability score. It's simple on purpose. If even this model can call your next topic 70% of the time, that's worth knowing.

Persona assignment uses k-means clustering on those metrics. Eight archetypes, each seeded with a hand-crafted centroid so the clusters stay interpretable: The Deep Diver, The Butterfly, The Algorithm's Darling, The Scholar, The Trend Chaser, The Ghost, The Loyalist, The Consumer.

The part I think about

Data Twin uses the same techniques as the systems it's making visible. The difference is you see the result and nothing gets kept. Whether that's enough is a real question.

High profilability isn't inherently bad — a focused researcher will score high too. But the same number in different hands is a targeting signal. The data doesn't change. The context does.

The model also can't tell you why you did anything. It doesn't know the Bali search was a fantasy. What it gives you is a compression — accurate in some ways, missing most of the point.

Built with FastAPI, React, DistilBERT, and a Markov chain. Open source. Data never leaves your session.
