what fashion week taught me about being in rooms you don't belong in

Operations Assistant · Fashion Tech at NYFW

I went to New York Fashion Week with no fashion background and a clipboard for the coat check. Operations assistant for a fashion tech company — managing the door, running logistics, working behind the bar, fixing whatever broke before each show.

On paper, fashion and tech don't have much in common. But backstage before a show, watching people hold everything together under pressure — it looked exactly like every startup crunch I've seen. Same chaos, same reliance on whoever's willing to do the unglamorous work without being asked, same culture of just figuring it out.

The aesthetics were different. The work ethic wasn't. People in both industries grind in the same way, care in the same way, and show up for each other in the same way. That wasn't something I expected to walk away thinking about.
