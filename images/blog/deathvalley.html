Death Valley · Hiking Trip

I left for Death Valley the day after graduation. Four years of constant motion, and then suddenly: salt flats, no signal, nowhere to be.

The landscape is genuinely absurd — 282 feet below sea level, mountains at the edges, nothing in between. We hiked Badwater Basin and Zabriskie Point and did the thing where you stand somewhere enormous and just sort of let it be enormous.

After months of finishing, it was the first time I wasn't trying to get anywhere. Turns out that's exactly what I needed.

I'd go back. Probably will.
